# 💫 About Me:
Founder & Product Engineer at **[Sadhya](https://sadhya.app)** — an AI-powered exam preparation platform for India's competitive exams (UPSC, JEE, NEET, SSC, BPSC).

I'm building Sadhya end to end: the product, the engineering and the AI underneath it — a tutor that answers from the official syllabus and shows its sources, practice tests and previous-year questions, and progress tracked against the syllabus.

Also an Assistant System Engineer at Tata Consultancy Services. MCA '24, Jawaharlal Nehru University. I enjoy algorithms, data structures and full-stack work with React, TypeScript and Node.js.

🚀 Try it at **[sadhya.app](https://sadhya.app)** — feedback and bug reports are always welcome.
